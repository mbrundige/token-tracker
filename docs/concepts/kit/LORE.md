### Why “florin”?

In 1252 Florence minted the *fiorino d’oro* — the gold florin. It kept its weight and purity for centuries, so merchants from London to the Mediterranean trusted it as a **unit of account**: the coin you settled in, not the coin you bought bread with.

Florentine banking houses (Bardi, Peruzzi, later the Medici) cleared big obligations with florins and *lettere di credito* — letters of credit — so gold did not have to travel with every deal. Their *libri di conto* (account books) were the shared truth.

**token-tracker** is that idea for AI. Tokens are the florins. Your *libro* lives in `~/.token-tracker/`. Every host (Cursor, Claude, Gemini, Codex…) is a *cambiatore* feeding one local ledger. The **F** is for *fiorino*. The blue chip is the modern assay mark: this unit is counted here, on your machine.

We don’t move your gold. We count what you already spent.

### Shorter blurb (one paragraph)

Florence’s gold florin (*fiorino d’oro*) was Europe’s trusted settlement coin — stable, named, believed. **token-tracker** treats AI tokens the same way: one local unit of account across agent hosts, with an **F** for *fiorino* and a shared *libro* under `~/.token-tracker/`.
