# 01 — Florin-Coin

## Thesis
A flat **F** on a reeded coin = your token unit of account.

## Craft note
Florentine florin (unit of account) → monogram disc, not Medici cosplay.

## Ground / accent
Bone or near-black · one accent `#2B6CFF` (EPROM window on the F).

## Assets
- Primary: `hero-icon.png` (hard F-coin, icon-ready)
- Alt: `alt-mascot-cut.png` (Nous-cut hand + F)
- SVG: `icon.svg`, `icon-mono.svg` (also in `../../svg/florin/`)

## Strengths
- Reads at ≤32px
- Ownable silhouette
- Ship path already has SVG lockups

## Kill risks
- Letter-F alone feels generic without coin rim
- “Florin” name can skim as crypto if metallic 3D creeps back
